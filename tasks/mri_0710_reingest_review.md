## 6. Why the folders were orphaned (the July failure)

**Established by the pattern, not by a log** (no GUI log survives on the NAS). On 2026-07-16 the frozen
`gjesus3_ingest.exe` had never completed a committed ingest: it **crashed at README generation** because `ingest/` was
not `sys._MEIPASS`-aware and `README_raw.txt` was not bundled (CHANGELOG 2026-07-17). The crash's own cleanup
(`_rollback_uncommitted`) then failed with `[WinError 5]`, because the operator's write-not-modify permission on
`raw\` blocks the delete (memory note, 2026-07-17). The orphans fit exactly: created in one minute (11:16), a
sidecar and `checksums.json` written, **no `README.txt`**, no registry row, ids reserved. The m6 attempts the same
day (counter 30) are the same failure. Fixed and redeployed 2026-07-17; the m1 session was ingested cleanly that day.
